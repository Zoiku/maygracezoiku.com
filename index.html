Maygrace Zoiku is a multifaceted professional who brings the same intentionality and rigour to every role she holds. She is the CEO and Founder of The Well Foundation, a Christian NGO empowering women and children across communities. Simultaneously, she serves as the Accountant for Widi Modern Technology Locks Ltd, the family's security door company based in East Legon, Ghana.

Her most recent chapter has seen her step into the world of data and technology — completing an intensive analytics programme where she co-led the development of an AI-powered finance tracker. Whether leading an organisation, managing accounts, or building data pipelines, Maygrace operates with the same quiet confidence and attention to detail.

Her faith is not separate from her work — it is the foundation of it.

The Well Foundation is a Christian non-governmental organisation founded by Maygrace Zoiku. Its mission is the empowerment of women and children — spiritually, economically, and socially — through faith-based initiatives that create sustainable, lasting change.

From skills development and humanitarian aid to spiritual formation, The Well Foundation meets people where they are and walks with them toward where they could be.

✦ Spiritual Empowerment Grounded in Christian values and faith
✦ Economic Empowerment Skills development and financial upliftment
✦ Social Empowerment Community, belonging, and mutual support
